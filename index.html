Neon Rush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Neon Rush ("the game") is developed by Job Haze Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game does and does not collect.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-up, and no servers of ours receive your data. Your game progress, scores, coins and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID and related technical information to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Third-party services

Apart from Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Play (distribution and billing infrastructure), the game does not share data with third parties.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
